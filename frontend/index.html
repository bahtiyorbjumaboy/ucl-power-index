Methodology & sources

Composite power score = 60% pre-season expert baseline + 40% UEFA club coefficient, then adjusted after every match by result margin and whether the outcome was an upset relative to the pre-matchday ranking. Movement shown is versus the pre-Matchday-1 baseline; swings of more than 5 places are flagged.

Expert baseline: aggregated from publicly available pre-season Champions League power rankings (Goal.com, ESPN, CBS Sports, Bleacher Report, Sports Illustrated).

Live results & standings: pulled from a public soccer scoreboard feed on a rolling schedule. UEFA coefficient: official club coefficient values.

Knockout outlook (Top-8%, Advance%) is a heuristic estimate from current rank and matchday performance, not a formal statistical projection.
